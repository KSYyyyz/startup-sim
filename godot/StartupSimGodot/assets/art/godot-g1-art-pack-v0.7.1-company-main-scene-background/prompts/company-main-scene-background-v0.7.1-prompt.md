# Prompt - company-main-scene-background-v0.7.1

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: Godot 2D company main scene background for a top-down office construction and management simulation game

Primary request: Create a polished company main scene background for Startup Sim, a top-down office-building business simulation. The image should be a usable game background, not a marketing illustration.

Scene/backdrop: A compact early-stage SaaS startup office seen from a clean top-down / slightly isometric game perspective. Include a readable rectangular office floor area with subtle grid-friendly floor tiles, low perimeter walls, windows along one side, an entrance/reception edge, and light architectural detail. Leave the central floor mostly open so Godot can place zones, facilities, employees, overlays, and UI on top.

Subject: The office environment itself: warm but professional workspace, early startup scale, visible room boundaries, empty buildable floor, tasteful hints of startup identity through shapes and materials only.

Style/medium: high-quality stylized 2D game art, clean painterly pixel-friendly rendering, compatible with existing top-down office atlases, crisp readable silhouettes, no photorealism.

Composition/framing: 16:9 landscape composition, camera looking down at the full office. Keep important floor area centered. Keep edges and corners visually finished but not busy. Preserve safe empty space near the right and bottom edges for future UI panels.

Lighting/mood: bright daytime office lighting, optimistic startup mood, soft ambient shadows, clear readability.

Color palette: balanced neutral office floors and walls with restrained blue, green, and warm accent colors; avoid a one-note purple, beige, brown, or dark-blue palette.

Materials/textures: tiled floor, glass windows, painted walls, subtle wall trim, door frame, minimal wiring/fixtures; no dense furniture clutter.

Text: none.

Constraints: no readable words, no logo text, no UI, no people, no large fixed furniture in the central buildable area, no watermark. The result should work as a background layer under separately placed office tiles, zone overlays, facilities, employees, and feedback FX.
