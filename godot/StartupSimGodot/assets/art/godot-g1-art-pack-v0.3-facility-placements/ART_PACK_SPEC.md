# Startup Sim Godot G1 Art Pack v0.3 - Facility Placements

Storage policy: tracked Godot art pack. Keep active Godot gameplay assets under `godot/StartupSimGodot/assets/art/`; the external `D:\美术资源\startup-sim\godot-g1-art-pack-v0.3-facility-placements` copy is only a backup.

## Goal

Provide a stricter facility placement and upgrade atlas for the playable office prototype. This pack is meant to support visible placement and upgrade feedback for desks, product planning, servers, sales workstations, meeting rooms, and rest/coffee facilities.

## Asset Group

- `facility-placement-atlas-v0.3`
  - Grid: 6 columns x 3 rows
  - Cell size: 256 x 342 px
  - Columns: desk, product whiteboard, server rack, sales call booth, meeting table, coffee/rest corner
  - Rows: level 1, level 2, level 3

## Notes

This pack should be treated as a placement-oriented upgrade over the broader v0.1 facility atlas. It is still prototype art, but the upgrade tiers are clearer and the six facility families map directly to near-term office-management needs.
