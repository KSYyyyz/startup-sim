# Prompt - employee-activity-state-atlas-v1.0

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: transparent-ready employee activity state sprite atlas for Godot top-down office management simulation

Primary request: Create a clean 8 columns x 4 rows sprite atlas of employee activity states for Startup Sim. Each cell is one centered small top-down / slightly isometric office worker sprite, readable as a game unit, with clear body pose and activity cue. The atlas must be usable for slicing into equal cells.

Canvas/layout: single atlas, exactly 8 columns and 4 rows, equal cells, consistent margins, each character centered with generous padding, no overlapping between cells. No labels, captions, letters, numbers, words, UI panels, or decorative text anywhere.

Scene/backdrop: Create all sprites on a perfectly flat solid #ff00ff chroma-key background for background removal. The background must be one uniform color with no shadows, gradients, texture, reflections, floor plane, or lighting variation. Do not use #ff00ff anywhere in the sprites.

Rows / employee variants from top to bottom, keep each row visually distinct:
row 1 product_engineer: young adult with rounder face, short dark hair, hoodie or casual startup jacket, laptop/tech cue, looking slightly down-left in some poses
row 2 sales_specialist: energetic middle-aged person with angular face, styled hair, blazer or smart casual outfit, headset/phone cue, looking slightly right or forward in some poses
row 3 ops_engineer: older calm person with square face, glasses, practical vest or dark work shirt, server/tool cue, looking slightly left/back in some poses
row 4 office_generalist: younger adult with different hairstyle, cardigan or colorful shirt, notebook/people-support cue, looking forward/down or side in some poses

Columns / activity states from left to right:
1 working_focus: seated or leaning into task, focused posture
2 rest_break: relaxed break posture with cup or stretch, no text
3 entertainment: small playful leisure cue like handheld game/music sparkle, no symbols or letters
4 restroom_need_away: stepping away / urgent away posture with small neutral door-like cue, no toilet graphic, no text
5 sick_leave: tired/sick posture with thermometer or blanket cue, no medical cross if it looks like text
6 training: learning posture with book/tablet/skill nodes, no letters on pages
7 emotional_pressure: stressed or upset posture with abstract stress particles, no punctuation marks
8 idle_walk: neutral standing/walking transition pose for office simulation

Style/medium: polished 2D stylized game sprites, crisp silhouettes, readable at small size, compatible with top-down office atlas art, soft antialiasing, no photorealism.

Character diversity constraints: employees must not all share the same face. Vary age, face shape, hair, outfit, accessories, expression, skin tone, pose, and facing direction. Do not make every character face right.

Color palette: varied but restrained office clothing and status accents; avoid dominant purple-only, beige-only, brown-only, or dark-blue-only palette.

Constraints: no readable text, no numbers, no watermark, no large UI cards, no cast shadows onto the background, no contact shadows, no reflections. Keep each sprite separated from the chroma-key background with crisp edges for clean alpha removal.
