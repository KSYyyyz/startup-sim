# office-tile-expansion-atlas-v0.4 Prompt

Create a clean game-ready tile atlas on a perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key background. The subject is office map construction tiles and placement guides for a top-down SaaS startup office simulation. Include carpet, concrete, wood, glass wall, solid wall, corner wall, door, window wall, cable hatch, server floor, meeting carpet, sales floor, product/R&D floor, rest floor, valid/invalid/selected/hover outlines, occupied marker, zone boundary edges and corners, expansion arrows, warning stripe, improvement sparkle, blocked, opportunity, risk, and transparent grid markers. No text, no watermark, no #00ff00 in tiles.

Production note: the generated source was padded to `1600 x 1000` so Godot can slice it as 8 x 5 cells of `200 x 200`.
