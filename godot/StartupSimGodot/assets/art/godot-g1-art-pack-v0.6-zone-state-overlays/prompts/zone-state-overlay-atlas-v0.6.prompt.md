# zone-state-overlay-atlas-v0.6 Prompt

Create a clean game-ready overlay sheet on a perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key background. The subject is visual overlays for office zones in a top-down SaaS startup office simulation: 6 columns for product/R&D, sales, server, meeting, recruiting, rest; 5 rows for normal, risk/pressure, opportunity, blocked, improving/recovery. Each cell is a translucent square or border treatment with a tiny abstract badge, no text, no watermark, readable on office tiles.

Production note: the generated source was padded to `1380 x 1150` so Godot can slice it as 6 x 5 cells of `230 x 230`.
