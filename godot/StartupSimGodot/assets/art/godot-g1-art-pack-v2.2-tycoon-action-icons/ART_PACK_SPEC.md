# Tycoon Action Icon Atlas v2.2

Version: 2.2

Purpose: Startup Sim Godot desktop HUD and object-panel icon expansion for business categories, object operations, and status hints.

Generated through the image-2 art route, then normalized into a transparent PNG atlas plus 224px, 64px, and 48px individual transparent icon exports.

Text policy: no localized labels, letters, numbers, months, KPI values, company names, or report text are baked into these assets. Use “现金流可支撑时间” in Godot UI copy.
