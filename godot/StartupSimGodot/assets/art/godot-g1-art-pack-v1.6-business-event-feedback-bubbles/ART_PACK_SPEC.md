# Business Event Feedback Bubble Atlas v1.6

Version: 1.6

Purpose: Startup Sim Godot employee bubbles, business events, floating metric FX, and review markers

Generated through the image-2 art route, then normalized into transparent PNG atlas and individual slices.

Text policy: do not bake Chinese, numbers, dates, months, KPI values, or user-facing labels into these art assets. Godot should render all readable text with Label or RichTextLabel nodes.
