# office-tile-atlas-v0.1

Create a sprite atlas for a Godot 2D top-down office construction game about running a SaaS startup. The atlas should contain clean, sliceable office tiles arranged in a regular grid on a perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key background.

Content: neutral office floor tile, carpet tile, server-room dark floor tile, meeting-room wood floor tile, rest-area warm floor tile, exterior wall straight piece, interior wall straight piece, wall corner piece, glass partition straight piece, glass corner piece, doorway, window segment, grid selection highlight, valid placement highlight, invalid placement highlight, hover outline.

Style: polished 2D management-game art with light pixel-art influence, top-down with slight isometric readability, crisp edges, consistent tile scale, no shadows outside each tile, no labels.

Layout: 4 columns by 4 rows, each sprite centered in its cell, equal padding, no overlapping.

Avoid: readable text, logos, watermark, realistic photography, clutter, perspective mismatch, gradients in the background.
