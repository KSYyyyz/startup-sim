# status-icon-atlas-v0.1

Create a clean icon atlas for Godot UI in a startup office management game. Arrange icons in a regular grid on a perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key background.

Content: cash, cash runway, MRR, users, product, reputation, equity, valuation, fatigue, mood, health, bathroom need, rest need, entertainment need, sick leave, high efficiency trait, fast learner trait, easily tired trait, emotional trait, late trait, communication trait, normal zone, risk zone, opportunity zone, blocked zone, improving zone, facility upgrade, salary cost, training, monthly report, achievement, competitor pressure, customer feedback.

Style: polished 2D game UI icons with light pixel-art influence, crisp silhouettes, readable at 32x32 and 64x64, simple multi-color palette, no text.

Layout: 8 columns by 4 rows, equal cell size, each icon centered, no labels, no overlapping.

Avoid: letters, numbers, readable text, logos, watermark, realistic photography, clutter.
