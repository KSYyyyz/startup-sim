# recruitment-portrait-sheet-v0.1

Create a recruitment portrait sheet for a Godot startup management game. Show twelve diverse employee candidate bust portraits arranged in a regular grid on a perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key background.

Characters should vary strongly in age, face shape, gender presentation, ethnicity, hairstyle, expression, outfit, and gaze direction. Include candidates for developer, backend engineer, AI researcher, product manager, designer, QA tester, DevOps, sales, customer success, HR, finance ops, and office admin.

Style: polished 2D game portrait art with light pixel-art influence, bright readable office-game palette, memorable faces, not generic corporate headshots.

Layout: 4 columns by 3 rows, each bust centered in its cell, equal padding, no labels, no overlapping.

Avoid: same-face repetition, all young characters, all same gaze direction, readable text, logos, watermark, realistic photography, clutter.
