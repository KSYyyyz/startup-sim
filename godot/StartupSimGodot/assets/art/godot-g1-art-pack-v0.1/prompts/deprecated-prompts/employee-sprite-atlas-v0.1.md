# employee-sprite-atlas-v0.1

Create a sprite atlas for Godot employee characters in a top-down office management game. Arrange small office-worker sprites in a regular grid on a perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key background.

Rows are roles and columns are states. Six rows, five columns:
1. developer: idle, working at laptop, walking, tired, resting.
2. sales rep: idle, calling customer, walking, tired, resting.
3. operations/product employee: idle, checking clipboard, walking, tired, resting.
4. server engineer: idle, fixing server, walking, tired, resting.
5. HR/recruiter: idle, interviewing, walking, tired, resting.
6. manager: idle, presenting, walking, tired, resting.

Each role should have distinct clothing silhouette, age/face variety, hair shape, and color accents. Sprites should be readable at small scale and suitable for simple Godot animation prototypes.

Style: polished 2D top-down / three-quarter top-down character sprites with light pixel-art influence, consistent scale, crisp silhouettes.

Layout: 5 columns by 6 rows, equal cell size, each sprite centered, no labels, no overlapping.

Avoid: same-face repetition, all characters looking the same direction, readable text, logos, watermark, realistic photography, clutter.
