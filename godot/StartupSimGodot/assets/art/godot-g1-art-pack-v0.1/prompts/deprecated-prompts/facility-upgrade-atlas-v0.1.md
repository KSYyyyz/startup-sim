# facility-upgrade-atlas-v0.1

Create a sprite atlas for Godot office facilities in a startup management game. Arrange each sprite in a clean grid on a perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key background.

Rows are facility families and columns are upgrade tiers. Six rows, three columns:
1. office desk: basic desk, dual-monitor efficient desk, premium ergonomic workstation.
2. product whiteboard: simple whiteboard, product war room board, advanced research wall.
3. sales phone booth: basic phone desk, sales pod, growth command station.
4. server rack: compact rack, server pair, upgraded reliability server cluster.
5. meeting table: small meeting table, board meeting table, fundraising conference setup.
6. coffee/rest facility: coffee machine, sofa coffee corner, upgraded rest lounge.

Style: polished 2D top-down or slight isometric management-game sprite art with light pixel-art influence, crisp silhouettes, visible upgrade differences, consistent scale.

Layout: 3 columns by 6 rows, equal cell size, each facility centered and fully inside its cell, no labels.

Avoid: readable text, logos, watermark, realistic photography, clutter, heavy shadows, background gradients.
