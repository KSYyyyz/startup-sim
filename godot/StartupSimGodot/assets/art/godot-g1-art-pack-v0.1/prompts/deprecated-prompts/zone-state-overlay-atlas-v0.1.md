# zone-state-overlay-atlas-v0.1

Create a sprite atlas for Godot zone overlays in a top-down office construction game. Arrange transparent-feeling overlay tiles and status markers in a clean grid on a perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key background.

Content: research zone overlay, sales zone overlay, server zone overlay, meeting zone overlay, recruiting zone overlay, market zone overlay, rest zone overlay, neutral zone overlay; status markers for normal, risk, opportunity, blocked, improving; small corner badges for low morale, cash pressure, product progress, customer growth.

Style: polished 2D management-game UI art with light pixel-art influence, semi-transparent color language, readable at small size, no text, no labels.

Layout: 4 columns by 4 rows, each sprite centered in its cell, consistent icon scale, equal padding, no overlapping.

Avoid: readable text, logos, watermark, realistic photography, clutter, baked UI labels.
