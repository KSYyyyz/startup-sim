Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Godot 2D employee sprite atlas for office simulation
Primary request: Create a sprite sheet of distinct startup employee sprites with varied roles and states.

Style/medium: Polished tiny 2D game sprites, top-down/slight 3/4 office view, light pixel-art influence, crisp edges, readable at 32-64 px.

Constraints: The background must be one uniform #00ff00 color with no shadows, gradients, texture, reflections, floor plane, or lighting variation. Do not use #00ff00 anywhere in the subject. No cast shadow, no contact shadow, no reflection, no watermark, no text, no labels, no numbers.
