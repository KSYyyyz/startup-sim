Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Godot recruitment candidate portrait sheet
Primary request: Create a portrait sheet of twelve visually distinct startup employee candidates for a hiring UI.
Scene/backdrop: Perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key background for background removal.
Subject: A clean 4 columns x 3 rows sheet of upper-body bust portraits. Each portrait is a different character.
Style/medium: Polished stylized 2D game portrait art, expressive but suitable for a business management sim.
Composition/framing: Bust portraits, shoulders up or chest up, each centered in its cell with generous padding, no overlap.
Character variety: Include clearly different ages from early 20s to late 50s; varied face shapes, jawlines, noses, eye shapes, hairstyles, facial hair where appropriate, body types, accessories, skin tones, and gender presentation.

Angle/gaze matrix: Mix front-facing, left 3/4, right 3/4, left profile, right profile, slightly downcast focus, and direct eye contact. Do not make all characters face or look right.

Expression variety: Confident, calm, skeptical, energetic, tired-but-capable, thoughtful, warm, focused, serious, amused, nervous, determined.
Constraints: The background must be one uniform #00ff00 color with no shadows, gradients, texture, reflections, floor plane, or lighting variation. Do not use #00ff00 anywhere in the subject. No cast shadow, no contact shadow, no reflection, no watermark, no text, no labels, no numbers.
