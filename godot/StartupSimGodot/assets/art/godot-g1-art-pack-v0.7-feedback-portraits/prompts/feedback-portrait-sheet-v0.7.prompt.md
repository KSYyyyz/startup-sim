# feedback-portrait-sheet-v0.7 Prompt

Create a clean game-ready portrait sheet on a perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key background. The subject is 4 columns x 3 rows of bust portraits for a SaaS startup management simulation: CFO, CTO, COO, board investor, early customer champion, angry churn-risk customer, competitor founder, market/media analyst, product advisor, enterprise buyer, recruiter/HR advisor, founder mentor. Characters must have clearly different age, face shape, hairstyle, skin tone, clothing, accessories, expression, and gaze direction. No text, no watermark, no #00ff00 in portraits.

Production note: the generated source was padded to `1536 x 1152` so Godot can slice it as 4 x 3 cells of `384 x 384`.
